DDF Funders Network

About this data

Each dot represents a funder that has co-funded a program with DDF, sized by endowment and colored by DDF program. Darker edges connect a funder to DDF. Lighter edges connect funders in the same program. Edge width is the combined size of shared programs (log scale); dashed means the amount was not disclosed. DDF is drawn at a fixed size. Endowment figures mix endowment and total assets from 2024 to 2026.

Dot area = endowment: $0.5B $5B $15B outline = not found Split circle = funds in more than one program

Hover a funder, then click it for more info. Click again, or click empty space, to clear.
